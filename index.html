On the line

After you answer, you'll hear muffled phone chatter with pauses for you to reply. It starts quiet, like an earpiece. Tap Speaker during the call to make it louder, or Hold to silence it.

Before you count on it
  • Keep the app in front with the screen on. If you lock the phone or switch apps, it can't ring until you come back to it.
  • Turn up media volume. Press a volume key and drag the slider; the ringer volume doesn't affect it.
  • Contacts and settings are saved on this phone, separately for each place you open it (inside Claude, the downloaded file, or the installed app).

The screen goes black while it waits. Press and hold it to cancel.
